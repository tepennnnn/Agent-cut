# Selection nesting and expansion — 2026-10-18

## User behavior and contracts

**Nest selected clips** creates a reusable child sequence from selected timeline media, then replaces that interval with a video item and linked audio when selected audio exists. Linked and grouped items are included automatically. The earliest selected start becomes child time zero; gaps and relative offsets remain intact. The parent keeps its timeline timing and export range. Internal transitions, wholly contained captions, and markers inside the interval move into the child. Original media files and library metadata remain untouched.

The shared v2 command is `sequence_nest_selection` with `sequenceId`, `name` (1–80 characters) and `itemIds` (1–200; expanded selection also capped at 200). At least one video item is required. A new virtual sequence source and new parent video/audio tracks are allocated deterministically by the reviewed plan. Original tracks remain available for expansion. The child is not automatically made active; select the nested item and use **Open source sequence** to edit it.

Current nested output is opaque SDR video/stereo audio. To preserve composition, every media item playing anywhere in the chosen interval must be selected, even on another track. Select both sides of internal transitions. The operation rejects intersecting adjustment spans, captions crossing a boundary, selected muted tracks, participating track audio processors, any solo or ducking, and locked affected tracks. Clip effects, masks/blends, audio processors, speed curves, reverse, freeze, grouping and clip automation move with their source items. Existing cycle/depth, source, track, sequence, asset and render-contribution limits still apply.

**Expand nested clips** replaces an unprocessed full-length selection nest with fresh copies of its child clips on the original parent tracks. The command is `sequence_unnest` with `sequenceId` and the nested video `itemId`. Linked audio must be present when the child has audio. Moving an intact linked nest is supported: expanded clip/annotation times follow its new start. Item, link, group, caption, marker and transition IDs are reallocated to avoid collisions. Internal links and transitions are rebound. The child sequence/library source and empty wrapper tracks remain; other instances of the child continue to work.

Expansion requires the original parent and destination tracks, matching parent/child canvas and frame rate, matching unmuted destination/source track mixes, full source range at speed 1, and original synchronized linked wrappers. Wrapper effects/automation, gain, processors, masks/blends, speed curves, reverse/freeze, groups, track mute/mix, attached transitions, intersecting other media/adjustments, solo/ducking, or changed child track layout reject rather than discard behavior. Child clip edits on the original child tracks are supported when the full-length wrapper still matches the child duration. New child tracks, child adjustment spans and cross-parent expansion remain open.

Both commands are classified as destructive structural edits, requiring `timeline_plan`, matching project identity/revision/plan, and `confirmPlanId`. A rejected batch commits nothing. Undo/redo restores the entire structural change in one step. The UI asks for confirmation and cancellation leaves the revision unchanged.

## Verification and limits

Four focused tests cover linked/group inclusion, internal transitions and annotations, source isolation, locks, unsafe expansion, fresh IDs/rebound companions, moving an intact nest, deterministic reviewed plans, persistence and undo/redo. A real FFmpeg fixture compares original, nested and expanded frames through a red/blue cut, a dissolve, leading/interior gaps and later unselected media, and checks audio level within encoding tolerances. This is a composed-output comparison, not lossless/sample-identical output.

Browser verification uses intercepted HTTP with the real Store/Renderer on synthetic data. It checks selection, cancellation and confirmation, composed preview, expansion and undo; see `selection-nesting-ui-proof.png` and `v2-ui-test-results.json`. Final full-suite/extracted-package counts and file hashes are recorded in `selection-nesting-2026-10-18-release.json`.

Nested video/audio uses the existing H.264/AAC child-render path, with black gaps and additional encoding/limiter stages. Transparent nesting, source-quality mastering, arbitrary processed/trimmed expansion, track-mix/ducking/adjustment transfer and unrestricted partial selections remain open. The existing live HTTP client test is sandbox-blocked. Static Premiere `cmd.clip.nestify` evidence identifies the installed command surface; its native defaults/runtime semantics have not been verified. This defines Agent Cut's own bounded contract, not original-version equivalence.

The release updates both existing app folders while preserving project-file hashes and configurations. It does not edit/migrate saved projects or restart the running editor. Restart the intended editor folder and reconnect MCP; `editor_info` reports `selection-nesting-2026-10-18`. The original overall workstreams remain unfinished.
